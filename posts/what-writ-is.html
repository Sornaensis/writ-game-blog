What Writ Is

Devlog #1 · 2026-11-22 #writ-online #blog

Writ is an idea that has been brewing in my mind for many years.

It is the idea that a game world, can be a real world. Not a world in the sense that there is a map, and some characters placed on it, and perhaps a neat set of scripted encounters. But a world, in the sense that the events happening in it, can be traced backwards causally to the material composition of it.

It is the idea that a persistent multiplayer online game with permanent death, can be both challenging and rewarding. That fun isn't tied to an arbitrary, fixed progression system.

It is the idea that setting things into motion, and watching the consquences unfold should feel incredibly satisfying. That cooperating with other players can be a natural element of the game, without introducing arbitrary mechanics to force it, and without taking away danger and risk.

It is the idea that, when I send my character off from one location to another, the things in between those two locations have a substantial impact on both the length of the journey as well as its risk.

It is the idea that a persistent multiplayer online game can work without becoming dominated solely by players who spend the most time logged into it. That a game world can have genuine exploration, and genuine history that isn't solely crafted by its writers.

It is the idea that combat does not need to be the primary, or even the secondary mechanism by which a game should operate, to be a fantasy role playing game.

It is the idea that allowing players to do and act as they please should be the highest priority of any game.
